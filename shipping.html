Shipping questions, answered.

Can you collect from my home?

Tell us your city and postcode. We confirm pickup availability, charges and a collection window before booking. Do not send stock to an office without acceptance instructions.

What can I send?

Describe every item accurately. Food, perfume, liquids, batteries, medicines and other restricted goods require carrier and destination checks before acceptance.

Are duties and customs included?

Not automatically. The written quote states who pays duties, import VAT and clearance charges, and which documentation is needed.

What if something is lost or damaged?

Ask about cover, exclusions, limits and claim deadlines before dispatch. Keep receipts and packing photos. We help coordinate enquiries; compensation follows the agreed carrier terms.
