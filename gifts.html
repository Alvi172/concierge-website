What you receive

Which package should I choose?

  1. Choose Thoughtful Delivery for one gift from one supplier, such as flowers or a cake, to one recipient. You approve the item and delivery charge.
  2. Choose Signature Surprise when the brief needs more coordination, such as flowers, a cake and a gift brought together into one agreed surprise. Includes up to two hours of planning, not two hours of event attendance.
  3. Choose Celebration Atelier for a small celebration involving up to three supplier bookings and a run sheet. A staffed event or elaborate decoration requires a separate quote.

How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Is the gift included in the service price?+

No. The service price pays for our planning and coordination. You also pay for the chosen gift, cake, flowers, delivery and any venue or supplier. Tell us your total budget so we can check whether the idea is realistic.

Can you surprise someone at home or work?+

Only where delivery and access are appropriate. We will not enter a home, contact an employer unnecessarily or expose private information to stage a surprise. A trusted recipient-side contact may be needed.

Can you organise halal food or an alcohol-free hamper?+

Include these requirements in the brief. We ask suppliers to confirm relevant ingredients or certification rather than assume every product is suitable. Serious allergy requirements must be confirmed directly with the food provider.

Can you promise midnight delivery or film the reaction?+

Only a supplier’s confirmed time window can be quoted. Filming requires the affected person’s permission and is not included automatically. We do not promise an exact surprise reaction or public feature.
