YOUR QUOTE, EXPLAINED

No mystery total.

Every quote separates the service fee, goods, third-party services, travel, shipping, insurance and any estimated customs charges. Any FX conversion rate and payment fee is disclosed before you approve.

For customs estimates, we identify who pays any difference when the final assessment arrives. Nothing is deliberately under-declared.

Standard scope rules

  • Fieldwork: two-hour minimum unless a package states otherwise.
  • Additional remote time: £55/hour, billed in agreed 15-minute increments.
  • Rush work within 48 hours: proposed 25% uplift, only if accepted and disclosed.
  • Out-of-hours work, extra waiting, travel time and mileage: separately quoted before booking.
  • Service changes require a revised quote. No automatic overage charges.
