In simple words

This is practical help for adult students who already have their travel permission and a place to stay. We help plan the journey from the airport, explain the first-week tasks and assist with agreed applications and appointments. You remain in control of your own documents and decisions.

What you receive

An agreed arrival plan, a personalised first-week checklist, practical assistance within your time allowance and a summary of completed and outstanding tasks.

Which package should I choose?

  1. Choose Arrival Essential if accommodation is already arranged and you mainly need a pre-arrival plan and someone to coordinate the transfer. This is remote coordination; a personal airport welcome is an extra.
  2. Choose First Week if the student needs help understanding banking documents, the local doctor, a phone plan and the National Insurance application process. Six hours are shared across these tasks; this is not six hours for each task.
  3. Choose Settle & Stay if help will be spread over the first month. The ten-hour allowance can be used for follow-up appointments, university admin and learning local services. It is not daily supervision.

How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

What are a GP and a “surgery”?+

A GP is a general-practice doctor. A GP surgery is the local doctor’s clinic, not an operation. We explain how to find and register with a suitable practice. In England, registration is available directly and does not generally require proof of address or immigration status.

What is National Insurance, and must I have a number before starting work?+

A National Insurance number helps record tax and contribution information. Eligibility depends on your situation; it is not a work permit. GOV.UK says you can start work before receiving the number if you can prove the right to work. We cannot change the work conditions on your visa.

Will you open the bank account for the student?+

We help check the bank’s document list, prepare for an appointment and explain the application steps. The student applies in their own name and completes identity checks. The bank can refuse an application; our fee is for the agreed assistance, not a guaranteed account.

Can parents book and pay from Pakistan?+

Yes, a parent can request a quote. An adult student must agree to assistance and to any sharing of their information with family. Payment arrangements are confirmed privately before acceptance.

Will you find a house and guarantee an NHS dentist?+

No. At launch we provide no-fee accommodation signposting rather than paid tenancy-finding. For dentists we search availability and explain options; waiting lists and treatment charges may apply.
