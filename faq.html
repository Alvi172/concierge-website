Getting started

What is Concierge.pk in simple words?+

It is a personal coordination service for people in Pakistan who need practical help in the UK. For example, you can ask about a student arrival, a gift for a relative, shopping or a business meeting. You tell us the task; we confirm whether we can help and quote the work.

How do I choose the correct service?+

Use “Find my service” and select the sentence closest to your situation. On the service page, read “Which package should I choose?”. If you still are not sure, select “Something else” in your enquiry and explain the task in ordinary words.

Do I need a membership or an account?+

No. You can request a one-off service. Signing up means registering interest and contact details; it is not a bank account, a seller account, a paid membership or a confirmed booking.

Can I enquire from Pakistan for someone in the UK?+

Yes. Tell us where you are and where the task must happen. For assistance involving another adult, their permission may be needed. A surprise gift still needs suitable delivery and access arrangements.

What should I send in my first enquiry?+

Your name, reply email, service, city/postcode, preferred date, approximate total budget and a short explanation. For shopping, add a product link. For a meeting, describe the goal. Do not send passports, banking passwords, OTPs or medical records.

Can I write my enquiry in Urdu?+

You can write your brief in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. We will confirm the communication arrangements for the accepted assignment. Official applications and third-party appointments may require English or a professional interpreter.

What happens after I submit a live form?+

We review the request, ask for any missing practical details and confirm whether there is capacity. If suitable, we provide a written scope and itemised quote. Submitting a form does not reserve a date or guarantee an outcome.

Is the Google signup and enquiry form live?+

Yes. Open the Google form from Contact or Join the circle, select a service and submit your details. You will see a confirmation after submission. The optional local draft builder only prepares text; copying a draft does not submit it.

Prices, budgets and membership

What exactly am I paying for?+

The service fee pays for the agreed planning, time and practical work. Goods, delivery, taxi fares, tickets, venue hire, accommodation and other supplier charges are separate unless the written quote specifically includes them.

Are the prices shown in pounds or Pakistani rupees?+

They are British pounds (GBP). Do not assume a fixed rupee equivalent. If a PKR payment route is offered, the written quote must show the conversion rate, date, fees and validity period. No Pakistan payment method is promised before it is confirmed.

What does “from £399” mean?+

£399 is the proposed starting service fee for the stated standard scope. More locations, longer hours or unusual requirements may cost more. Your final quote specifies the actual fee before you agree. It is not permission to charge an undisclosed extra.

How can I compare a competitor’s price fairly?+

Check included hours, transport, shopping allowance, geography, number of appointments, reporting and ongoing support. A £499 package with a transport allowance is not identical to £399 plus a separately charged vehicle. Compare the final scope and total, not only the headline.

How do hours spread over several days work?+

Six hours over seven days means six total work hours used during that week. Preparation, calls, messages and reports use the allowance. It is not six hours every day, and it does not include round-the-clock availability.

What if the work takes longer?+

We explain the remaining work and ask you to approve more time or a smaller scope. The general remote rate is £55/hour where applicable; specialist work may use its own quote. We do not automatically keep billing without agreement.

Will I pay a membership fee and the full package fee for the same work?+

The same coordination work should not be billed twice. Your quote explains what uses your membership allowance and what is separately charged. Membership does not automatically include field visits, shopping commissions or supplier spending.

Which membership is right for me?+

For an occasional request, start without membership. Select is £19/month with 20 remote minutes; Family is £79 with 90 remote minutes; Private Desk is £299 with five remote hours. Choose based on repeat coordination needs, not an expectation of unlimited free tasks.

How do payments, cancellation and refunds work?+

The accepted quote identifies the contracting business, approved payment route, due date and cancellation conditions. Goods may need advance funding. Applicable statutory rights remain intact; authorised completed work and supplier bookings may affect refunds. Never send money to unverified account details.

Do you offer discounts for several jobs?+

Possibly where combining work actually saves time: the same shopping visit, arrival route or gift design, for example. We quote a defined group scope. Separate cities or urgent deadlines can increase the price instead.

Delivery, privacy and practical limits

Do you serve every UK city and airport?+

Not automatically. Give us the location and date so we can confirm availability and travel cost. A UK service menu does not mean there is a permanent team in each city.

How early should I contact you?+

Suggested lead times are shown on each service page. Allow roughly a week or more for arrivals and meetings, several days for ordinary gifts, and several weeks for events or overseas travel. These are planning suggestions, not guaranteed turnaround times.

Can you help urgently or outside normal hours?+

Only if expressly accepted. A proposed 25% rush uplift may apply for work requested within 48 hours. Waiting, late-night work and travel must be quoted separately. There is no general 24/7 or emergency service.

Will you update my family or business partner?+

Only within the permissions and recipients agreed for the task. An adult student’s parent does not automatically receive private information. Business reports go to authorised contacts.

Will I automatically appear in the Hall of Fame?+

No. It is an optional feature after completion. We separately agree the name, wording and photograph, if any. You can choose a private service with no mention. No children’s photos are published at launch.

What if you cannot complete the task?+

We explain what happened and distinguish work already completed from unused scope and supplier commitments. Any revised plan or refund is assessed under the agreed terms and applicable rights. An application refusal is different from failing to provide the promised assistance.

Is this a visa, legal, medical or investment service?+

No. We can coordinate appropriate professional appointments and some practical administration. Qualified or regulated providers handle advice and decisions in those areas. We do not promise visas, investment returns, bank approval or medical outcomes.

Student arrivals

What are a GP and a “surgery”?+

A GP is a general-practice doctor. A GP surgery is the local doctor’s clinic, not an operation. We explain how to find and register with a suitable practice. In England, registration is available directly and does not generally require proof of address or immigration status.

What is National Insurance, and must I have a number before starting work?+

A National Insurance number helps record tax and contribution information. Eligibility depends on your situation; it is not a work permit. GOV.UK says you can start work before receiving the number if you can prove the right to work. We cannot change the work conditions on your visa.

Will you open the bank account for the student?+

We help check the bank’s document list, prepare for an appointment and explain the application steps. The student applies in their own name and completes identity checks. The bank can refuse an application; our fee is for the agreed assistance, not a guaranteed account.

Can parents book and pay from Pakistan?+

Yes, a parent can request a quote. An adult student must agree to assistance and to any sharing of their information with family. Payment arrangements are confirmed privately before acceptance.

Will you find a house and guarantee an NHS dentist?+

No. At launch we provide no-fee accommodation signposting rather than paid tenancy-finding. For dentists we search availability and explain options; waiting lists and treatment charges may apply.

Gifts & surprises

Is the gift included in the service price?+

No. The service price pays for our planning and coordination. You also pay for the chosen gift, cake, flowers, delivery and any venue or supplier. Tell us your total budget so we can check whether the idea is realistic.

Can you surprise someone at home or work?+

Only where delivery and access are appropriate. We will not enter a home, contact an employer unnecessarily or expose private information to stage a surprise. A trusted recipient-side contact may be needed.

Can you organise halal food or an alcohol-free hamper?+

Include these requirements in the brief. We ask suppliers to confirm relevant ingredients or certification rather than assume every product is suitable. Serious allergy requirements must be confirmed directly with the food provider.

Can you promise midnight delivery or film the reaction?+

Only a supplier’s confirmed time window can be quoted. Filming requires the affected person’s permission and is not included automatically. We do not promise an exact surprise reaction or public feature.

Business representation

Can you negotiate or sign a contract on my behalf?+

We can communicate your approved position and report the response. We do not sign or commit your business unless a separate lawful authority and scope have been reviewed and accepted. Ordinary packages do not include this authority.

Does a supplier visit prove the supplier is genuine?+

No single visit proves that. We can document what was observed and what public records show. Financial, legal, technical and fraud due diligence may need qualified specialists.

Can you interview a job applicant for me?+

We can ask a pre-agreed, appropriate question set and provide factual notes with the candidate’s knowledge. You make hiring decisions. Background investigations, discriminatory screening or unapproved recording are not included.

What legal or civil work is available?+

Document organisation and appointment coordination can be scoped. Solicitors, notaries and other appropriate professionals handle advice, court work and certification. We do not act as your lawyer or immigration adviser.

Seller & fulfilment desk

What are 3PL, FBA, FBM, SKU and pick-and-pack?+

3PL means a third-party warehouse/logistics provider. With FBA, Amazon stores and sends eligible stock; with FBM, you or your warehouse sends orders. A SKU is your identifier for a product or variant. Pick-and-pack means selecting the sold item, packing it and preparing it for delivery.

Can I buy a UK Amazon/eBay account or use your bank account?+

No. Accounts must accurately identify their true owner and business. We do not rent accounts, lend identity documents, provide false residence evidence or receive your marketplace income through our personal account.

Does forming a UK company guarantee approval?+

No. Company formation does not guarantee a bank account, marketplace acceptance, visa, tax benefit or sales. An appropriate formation/accounting provider must explain the actual obligations.

Can you give me a warehouse address now?+

Only after the products, owner, importer, storage arrangements, insurance and partner approval are checked. Shipping stock to an unapproved address can create serious costs and delays.

Why is your returns fee higher than a basic warehouse rate?+

Our proposed managed rate includes coordination and visual grading within the stated scope. Basic high-volume warehouse processing can cost less. We should only recommend the managed option when you need that extra work; repairs, complex authentication and lengthy investigations are extra.

Do you handle customs, VAT and product safety?+

We coordinate the questions and specialist introductions. Your written arrangement must identify the importer and responsible parties. Customs brokerage, tax advice and safety assessment are professional services, not automatically included in our fee.

Freelancer representation

Will you pretend to be my UK employee?+

No. A professional external representative can provide useful presence without a false identity or misleading business location. The relationship is disclosed.

Can you guarantee the client will award me the job?+

No. We can improve preparation and carry out the agreed meeting, but the buyer decides. The service fee covers work performed, not a sales outcome.

Can you receive my client payments or provide verification OTPs?+

No payment-routing or rented verification service is offered. A SIM or eSIM must be customer-owned and provider-approved. A phone number is not proof of UK residence or business eligibility.

Personal shopping

Do I pay both a fixed session fee and a percentage?+

Not within a stated Live Shop or Private Shopping Day basket limit. Those fixed packages include the purchase coordination. Shop & Send uses its percentage/minimum instead. Larger or more complex baskets receive a new quote.

What is landed cost?+

It is the total cost to get the item to you: goods, our service, delivery, insurance, customs duties/taxes and any applicable clearance or currency/payment charges. Customs estimates can differ from final assessment.

Can you buy from Harrods, Selfridges, Bicester Village or normal shops?+

Subject to stock, appointments and permissions, yes. The appropriate package depends on stores, time and basket value. Retailer names describe destinations; they do not mean we are their official partner.

Can you buy an iPhone that never needs PTA registration?+

We do not offer that promise. eSIM is a way to activate a phone plan, not a universal registration or tax exemption. Check the exact device, lawful network use, temporary-visitor eligibility and current PTA/FBR rules before buying.

What if I receive the wrong size, want a return or the parcel is damaged?+

Contact the agreed support route promptly, retain packaging and document the issue. Retailer conditions, our agreed agency/reseller role, carrier insurance and applicable consumer rights determine the next steps. International return postage can be expensive; eligible coordination starts at £25 per item.

Can you declare a lower price or mark a purchase as a gift to avoid duty?+

No. Customs declarations must be accurate. We can compare lawful shipping options but will not misdescribe the goods or their value.

Global assignments

Why is a flight not included in the day rate?+

Airfare, hotel and local transport depend heavily on dates and destination. Separating them lets you see the service cost and approve the actual trip budget.

Can you travel anywhere at short notice?+

No. Entry permission, permitted work activities, insurance, safety and schedule all matter. A visitor visa does not automatically allow paid local tasks.

Will you carry a parcel or cash for me?+

No unknown parcels or cash-transfer assignments. Accepted goods require verified contents, lawful import/export arrangements, carrier acceptance and suitable insurance.

Bespoke travel planning

Are flights, hotels or visas included?+

No. The package buys itinerary research and the stated revisions. Flights, hotels, travel insurance, attraction tickets, visas and meals are extra.

Can you book everything for me?+

Booking assistance depends on the accepted scope and appropriate travel-provider arrangement. At launch the default is direct customer booking or a suitable specialist. We do not present Concierge.pk as an ATOL-protected holiday organiser.

Can you plan for halal food, children or sensory preferences?+

Yes, describe the practical preferences so they inform the plan. Supplier suitability must be confirmed. We do not provide clinical advice or guarantee that a location will meet every access need.

What counts as a revision?+

A revision is one consolidated set of changes within the original destination, duration and traveller scope. Changing countries, doubling the trip or starting again with new dates can require a new quote.
