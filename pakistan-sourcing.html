Your sourcing questions.

Are you the manufacturer?

No. We coordinate sourcing and local support on your behalf. The quote identifies our role, supplier payments and any disclosed referral arrangement. You choose the supplier and approve purchasing commitments.

Is a pre-shipment audit a guarantee?

No. An agreed-scope inspection reports findings at a particular time, often using a sample. It cannot guarantee every unit or future performance. We distinguish visual checks from accredited audits, laboratory tests and regulatory certification.

Can you help a first-time buyer with a small order?

Yes. We help clarify specifications and explore realistic minimum quantities. A low-volume order may suit a trader or stock supplier rather than direct manufacturing; we explain the trade-offs.

Will you negotiate and protect my design?

We can request revised quotations and coordinate an NDA or manufacturing agreement for your lawyer to review. Share only the information needed at each stage. We cannot guarantee intellectual-property protection.

Can you arrange export and import paperwork?

We coordinate introductions and document collection with exporters, freight forwarders and customs agents. Qualified professionals must confirm commodity codes, duties, import restrictions and destination compliance.

Can you check surgical instruments or other regulated products?

We can help find suppliers and coordinate specialist checks. A factory visit does not establish product safety, medical-device approval or compliance. Use qualified technical and regulatory experts before purchase or import.

What should I send in my enquiry?

Send the product description or photo, quantity, specification, target budget, destination country, deadline and whether you need samples, a supplier visit, an inspection or full coordination. Do not send banking credentials or identity documents in the initial enquiry.
