How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Will you pretend to be my UK employee?+

No. A professional external representative can provide useful presence without a false identity or misleading business location. The relationship is disclosed.

Can you guarantee the client will award me the job?+

No. We can improve preparation and carry out the agreed meeting, but the buyer decides. The service fee covers work performed, not a sales outcome.

Can you receive my client payments or provide verification OTPs?+

No payment-routing or rented verification service is offered. A SIM or eSIM must be customer-owned and provider-approved. A phone number is not proof of UK residence or business eligibility.
