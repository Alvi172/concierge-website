Venue selection & visits

Compare country houses, hotels, gardens, marquees and banquet spaces. We coordinate availability checks, capacities, site visits or virtual tours, access, guest rooms and an indoor weather alternative.

Design, décor & getting the venue ready

Agree a colour palette and event mood, then coordinate florals, stage or mandap-style staging as requested, nikah seating, lighting, tableware, signage, welcome displays, setup and breakdown schedules.

Catering, cake & hospitality

Coordinate menus, tastings, halal requirements, allergens, children’s meals, serving style, refreshments and cake design. Suppliers confirm food safety, dietary handling and venue permissions.

Photography & film

Shortlist photographers and videographers by style and budget. Agree coverage hours, key family shots, privacy preferences, editing, albums, delivery dates and usage rights. Drone filming is subject to venue and local permissions.

Mehndi, nikah, baraat & walima

Plan separate or combined celebrations around your family traditions. Coordinate celebrant introductions where appropriate, performers, bridal preparation and a clear sequence for each event.

Guests, travel & accommodation

Coordinate room options, welcome packs, airport-transfer quotations, accessibility, children’s needs and guest communications. Travel suppliers confirm availability and terms; visas and entry decisions remain with the authorities.

Beauty, outfits & personal details

Arrange makeup and hair proposals, henna artists, outfit fittings, accessories, gift hampers and last-minute errands. Pakistan shopping support can complement a wedding held overseas.

Event-day coordination

Agree an on-site team, supplier contacts, arrival schedule, seating plan, rehearsal, setup checks and contingency plan. Attendance, staff numbers and working hours are explicitly included in your quote.

Before you say “book it”.

Is the marriage legally valid at any venue?

Legal marriage and a symbolic or religious celebration are different. Confirm notice, residency, registration and document requirements with the relevant registrar or local authority before booking. We coordinate introductions, not legal advice.

Can you plan from abroad?

Yes, ask for remote planning with video consultations and an agreed update schedule. Site visits and local attendance depend on confirmed coverage and are quoted.

Can you include female photographers, halal catering or private spaces?

Tell us your requirements at the start. We include them in supplier briefs and seek written confirmation; availability is checked before booking.

How do you protect our budget?

We agree priorities and a budget framework, compare itemised supplier proposals and flag exclusions. Exchange rates, changes, overtime and weather contingencies are considered before commitments.

Are you affiliated with the venues you research?

No partnership is implied. We use public venue information to inform shortlists and seek live quotations for your dates. The signed proposal identifies the actual suppliers and responsibilities.
