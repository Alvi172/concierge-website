In simple words

Start with the task, not a flight booking. We check whether it can be done remotely, by a suitable local provider or through dedicated travel. Overseas services are individually accepted; there is no permanent Concierge.pk team in every country.

What you receive

First, a feasibility brief; if accepted, a written itinerary, authority limits, budget and specified completion evidence. A third party’s cooperation is not guaranteed.

Which package should I choose?

  1. Choose Mission Scoping to compare realistic ways to complete the task before committing to an expensive journey. The fee is credited if the assignment is accepted within 30 days.
  2. Choose Local Partner Mission when an appropriate provider already near the location can perform the task. This is often cheaper than sending someone from the UK.
  3. Choose Dedicated Travel Mission when physical attendance by an assigned person is justified. Task days, travel-only days and trip expenses are separate in the quote.

Before we begin

  • Entry as a visitor does not automatically permit performing paid work. Each assignment is accepted only after immigration, work, insurance and local licensing checks.
  • No carrying unknown parcels, cash movement, undeclared goods, visa facilitation promises or personal substitution at identity appointments.
  • A trip is not booked until scope, cancellation rules, travel permissions, expense budget and a fallback are approved.

How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Why is a flight not included in the day rate?+

Airfare, hotel and local transport depend heavily on dates and destination. Separating them lets you see the service cost and approve the actual trip budget.

Can you travel anywhere at short notice?+

No. Entry permission, permitted work activities, insurance, safety and schedule all matter. A visitor visa does not automatically allow paid local tasks.

Will you carry a parcel or cash for me?+

No unknown parcels or cash-transfer assignments. Accepted goods require verified contents, lawful import/export arrangements, carrier acceptance and suitable insurance.
