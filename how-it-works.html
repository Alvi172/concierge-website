FROM YOUR FIRST IDEA TO THE FINAL DETAIL

A personal service.
A clear process.

The simplest way to begin is with one task, one location and a realistic budget.

01

Share a brief

Choose a service. Tell us the destination, date, desired outcome and approximate budget. Don’t send passports, bank details or private medical information in an initial enquiry.

02

We check the fit

Confirm availability, the delivery area, access permissions and whether a specialist is required. We can decline tasks outside our capacity or remit.

03

Agree the quote

Review the scope, time cap, service fee, external spend, cancellation conditions and who contracts with each supplier. Purchases need cleared funding and explicit approval.

04

Stay in the loop

Receive updates at agreed milestones. Changes and extra costs come back to you for approval.

05

Close the loop

Receive a completion summary and relevant receipts. Choose separately whether to share your experience publicly.
