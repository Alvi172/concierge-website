A brief, made real.

Illustration: a standard Returns Desk month with 40 items costs £79 + (40 × £5.50) = £299, before transport/storage. At higher volume, request a direct 3PL comparison: specialist warehouses may be cheaper for routine handling.

Before we begin

  • Do not send goods until a warehouse, acceptance terms, importer, insurance and customs responsibilities are confirmed in writing.
  • Qualifying storage of overseas-owned goods may require HMRC Fulfilment House Due Diligence Scheme approval. Initial fulfilment is partner-delivered, not an owned warehouse claim.
  • We do not sell UK identities, bank accounts, marketplace accounts, addresses that misrepresent residence or OTP services. Platforms and banks independently approve applicants.
  • Company formation, registered-office provision, tax and bookkeeping require a reviewed specialist model. No nominees, bank-access promises or sales guarantees.

How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

What are 3PL, FBA, FBM, SKU and pick-and-pack?+

3PL means a third-party warehouse/logistics provider. With FBA, Amazon stores and sends eligible stock; with FBM, you or your warehouse sends orders. A SKU is your identifier for a product or variant. Pick-and-pack means selecting the sold item, packing it and preparing it for delivery.

Can I buy a UK Amazon/eBay account or use your bank account?+

No. Accounts must accurately identify their true owner and business. We do not rent accounts, lend identity documents, provide false residence evidence or receive your marketplace income through our personal account.

Does forming a UK company guarantee approval?+

No. Company formation does not guarantee a bank account, marketplace acceptance, visa, tax benefit or sales. An appropriate formation/accounting provider must explain the actual obligations.

Can you give me a warehouse address now?+

Only after the products, owner, importer, storage arrangements, insurance and partner approval are checked. Shipping stock to an unapproved address can create serious costs and delays.

Why is your returns fee higher than a basic warehouse rate?+

Our proposed managed rate includes coordination and visual grading within the stated scope. Basic high-volume warehouse processing can cost less. We should only recommend the managed option when you need that extra work; repairs, complex authentication and lengthy investigations are extra.

Do you handle customs, VAT and product safety?+

We coordinate the questions and specialist introductions. Your written arrangement must identify the importer and responsible parties. Customs brokerage, tax advice and safety assessment are professional services, not automatically included in our fee.
