Before you buy.

Will I get a local number or bank verification texts?

These are travel-data plans unless a package explicitly includes calls or SMS. Do not buy a data-only plan expecting a UK number, banking OTPs or account-opening eligibility.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp?

You can normally use your existing WhatsApp account over eSIM data. Keep access to your original number if WhatsApp needs to verify it again.

Does an eSIM avoid PTA registration?

No general exemption is promised. Device registration and network rules are separate from purchasing an eSIM. Check current requirements for your device and visit.

Is unlimited data unrestricted?

Unlimited packages may have fair-use, speed, hotspot or other limits. Check the selected plan terms before purchasing.

Can I buy for family or employees?

Yes. Tell us the number of compatible devices, destinations and dates for a coordinated quote. Each device may need its own eSIM.

Can I top up or get a refund?

Rules depend on the selected package and whether it has been installed, activated or used. Confirm expiry, top-up availability and refund terms before payment.
