“My son or daughter is moving from Pakistan to the UK. We need help with arrival and everyday arrangements.”

This is practical help for adult students who already have their travel permission and a place to stay. We help plan the journey from the airport, explain the first-week tasks and assist with agreed applications and appointments. You remain in control of your own documents and decisions.

Start with Arrival Essential: £99. External costs are separate.

“I have products in Pakistan and want to sell them to UK customers, with help handling stock, parcels and returns.”

پاکستانی برانڈز کے لیے برطانیہ میں آن لائن فروخت، پارسل اور واپسی کے انتظام میں مدد۔

Think of this as a coordination desk between your business, online marketplace and an appropriate warehouse. We help map the work and manage agreed tasks. Your business owns its seller account, stock and customer funds. This is not a ready-made Amazon business or a guaranteed sales service.

06 / PERSONAL SHOPPING

“I want something from a UK shop, but I am in Pakistan and need someone to check it, buy it or arrange delivery.”

برطانیہ کی دکان سے پسندیدہ چیز دیکھنے، خریدنے اور پاکستان بھجوانے کے انتظام میں مدد۔

Send a product link, photo or clear wishlist. We confirm whether it can be bought and shipped lawfully, agree the service fee and ask you to approve the purchase. For a live visit, we show available items over a video call only where the shop permits it. Product costs and international delivery are separate.

Start with Shop & Send: 10% · min £29. External costs are separate.
