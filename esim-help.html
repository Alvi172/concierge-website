What is an eSIM?

A digital SIM profile installed on a compatible device. It lets you connect using a purchased plan without inserting a physical SIM.

How do I buy?

Search our catalogue, choose a package and open the WhatsApp enquiry. We confirm the plan, compatibility, availability and payment instructions. Clicking the button does not take payment.

How will I receive installation details?

We agree delivery of your QR code or activation instructions during order confirmation. Do not assume immediate automated delivery or an app account.

Is there a Concierge eSIM app?

This service uses our website and WhatsApp desk. There is no Concierge app, rewards wallet or automatic dashboard promised here.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

Usually yes: using another data connection does not require changing your WhatsApp number. Keep access to your original number if you later need to verify the account.

Are calls and SMS included?

Treat the catalogue as travel data unless your confirmed plan explicitly includes calls, texts or a telephone number. Internet calling uses data and depends on local availability.

Will my bank OTP arrive?

A data-only eSIM does not receive SMS sent to your home number. Keep an appropriate home-line arrangement and check its roaming charges and reception.

When does validity begin?

The trigger depends on the package. Ask whether installation, activation or first connection starts the clock before installing.

Can I install while abroad?

You need internet to download the profile. Install at a suitable time under your plan’s activation rules; airport Wi-Fi may be unreliable.

Can I scan a QR code on the same phone?

Use another screen or a printed copy. Some operating systems support other import methods; otherwise ask for manual installation details.

Can I use two SIMs together?

Dual-SIM capability and active-line limits depend on the device. Select the travel eSIM for data and review your home carrier’s charges for any line left enabled.

Is unlimited data unlimited at full speed?

Not necessarily. Fair-use thresholds, daily high-speed allowances, throttling and hotspot restrictions can apply. Confirm the exact plan before paying.

Can I use a hotspot?

Only where the package and device allow tethering. Ask before relying on it for a laptop or family group.

Will it work everywhere in Pakistan?

Coverage depends on partner networks, location, terrain, congestion and device support. Rural and remote coverage can vary; no nationwide uninterrupted-service guarantee is offered.

What about the UK and other destinations?

Search 202 country, territory and regional catalogue entries, including Pakistan and the UK. These are not 202 individual countries. A regional package covers only its specified destinations.

Does this bypass PTA?

It is not PTA approval, a carrier unlock or a guaranteed bypass. Eligible devices may use a foreign roaming eSIM under applicable rules; local Pakistani SIM registration is a separate matter. Confirm current eligibility before purchase.

Can a carrier-locked phone use it?

Not usually with an unrelated provider. Contact the original carrier about unlocking. Installing an eSIM does not remove a carrier lock.

Do I need data roaming enabled?

Travel eSIMs often require it on the travel line. Follow your plan instructions, while keeping home-line data roaming off if you want to avoid home-network data charges.

No connection after installation?

Check destination coverage, validity, remaining data, selected data SIM and roaming settings. Toggle airplane mode, restart and check the supplied APN or supported network. Do not delete the eSIM as a first troubleshooting step.

The QR code says already used or invalid. What next?

Stop repeated attempts. Contact us with the error message and order reference, hiding the QR code and personal identifiers. Many profiles have installation limits.

My internet is slow. What can I do?

Check signal, location, remaining allowance and fair-use restrictions. Try another permitted network if the plan allows manual selection. Coverage and speed are not guaranteed.

How do I check remaining data?

Ask which usage-reporting method is available for your confirmed plan. Your phone’s counter is an estimate and may not match the provider’s billing period.

Can I top up?

Top-up availability varies. Ask before the allowance expires; some plans require a new profile or replacement purchase.

Can I move it to another phone?

Transfers and reinstallation are not universal. Ask before changing device, deleting the profile or performing a factory reset.

Can I get a refund?

Eligibility depends on the agreed terms, activation status and fault investigation, subject to applicable consumer rights. Ask for cancellation and refund terms before payment.

Can family members share one QR code?

Do not assume so. Profiles are generally intended for one compatible device. Ask for a separate plan for each traveller or confirm permitted hotspot use.

What should I send support?

Your order reference, destination, model, time of issue and a redacted screenshot of the error. Never share passwords, bank credentials or activation codes publicly.
