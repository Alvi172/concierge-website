This notice covers the live Google enquiry form and the optional local draft builder. Full contracting details will be supplied before any paid service.

What this preview does

The enquiry composer runs in your browser. It does not submit or retain your form entries on our servers. Copying is your choice. No analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies have been added. Hosting may process technical request logs under its own policies.

Our live Google form

Concierge.pk uses Google Forms to receive service enquiries and optional update requests. Use the Contact form owner button at the bottom of the Google form to raise a privacy enquiry. Initial enquiries should contain name, reply details, service, approximate location, timing and a short brief. Do not upload passports, payment details or sensitive health information. Google processes submitted form data under its applicable terms and privacy arrangements.

Purpose and access

Contact information is used to respond to an enquiry and take requested pre-contract steps. Contract delivery, legal obligations and security may require additional processing. Only necessary information should be shared with the named people delivering the agreed task.

Marketing and publication

Marketing is optional and separate from service correspondence. Hall of Fame publication requires a separate, specific permission for the chosen name, image and words. The default is no public feature.

Retention and rights

Proposed policy: delete unconverted enquiries after 90 days unless a clear reason justifies longer retention. Contract and accounting records follow applicable legal retention requirements. The final notice will identify the controller, privacy contact, processors, international transfer arrangements and how to request access, correction or deletion and complain to the ICO.

Business disclosures before paid work

Business identity, contact details, actual processor arrangements and retention policy require confirmation. The Google form accepts enquiries. The website draft builder does not submit personal data.

WhatsApp enquiries

Contact Concierge.pk on +92 339 2334242 for service or privacy enquiries. WhatsApp links prepare a message for you to review and send, including the selected eSIM package where applicable. WhatsApp processes messages under its own terms. Do not include passwords, identity documents, financial records or health details in your initial message.
