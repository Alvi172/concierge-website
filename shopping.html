What you receive

Approved purchases, relevant retailer receipts, an arrival check and agreed dispatch instructions. Receipt evidence does not replace specialist authentication for collectibles or luxury resale.

Which package should I choose?

  1. Choose Shop & Send when you already know the items and retailer. The fee is 10% of the goods price, with a £29 minimum, for the stated basket limits.
  2. Choose Live Shop when you want up to one hour in a single shop to inspect and compare items remotely. The £99 fee replaces the purchase commission within the package’s £500 basket limit.
  3. Choose Private Shopping Day for up to four hours in one area and a basket up to £2,000. This is useful for several planned purchases; travel and shipment are additional.

IN PRACTICE

A brief, made real.

For £300 of goods, Shop & Send is £30: £330 before shipping, insurance, customs and other agreed costs. For £100 of goods the minimum fee is £29, so £129 before those extra costs. A small purchase may not justify international shipping.

Before we begin

  • Shipping, import duties, taxes, currency costs, insurance and restricted-goods handling are quoted separately. No under-declaration or false gift descriptions.
  • Harrods means the Knightsbridge department store; H beauty has other locations. We are independent and not affiliated with named retailers.
  • An eSIM is not a universal PTA exemption. Device model, lock status, warranty, battery-carriage rules and lawful Pakistan use must be checked before purchase.
  • Buyers approve substitutions. For high-value purchases, the accepted quote sets the cancellation, retailer return and agency/reseller position.

How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Do I pay both a fixed session fee and a percentage?+

Not within a stated Live Shop or Private Shopping Day basket limit. Those fixed packages include the purchase coordination. Shop & Send uses its percentage/minimum instead. Larger or more complex baskets receive a new quote.

What is landed cost?+

It is the total cost to get the item to you: goods, our service, delivery, insurance, customs duties/taxes and any applicable clearance or currency/payment charges. Customs estimates can differ from final assessment.

Can you buy from Harrods, Selfridges, Bicester Village or normal shops?+

Subject to stock, appointments and permissions, yes. The appropriate package depends on stores, time and basket value. Retailer names describe destinations; they do not mean we are their official partner.

Can you buy an iPhone that never needs PTA registration?+

We do not offer that promise. eSIM is a way to activate a phone plan, not a universal registration or tax exemption. Check the exact device, lawful network use, temporary-visitor eligibility and current PTA/FBR rules before buying.

What if I receive the wrong size, want a return or the parcel is damaged?+

Contact the agreed support route promptly, retain packaging and document the issue. Retailer conditions, our agreed agency/reseller role, carrier insurance and applicable consumer rights determine the next steps. International return postage can be expensive; eligible coordination starts at £25 per item.

Can you declare a lower price or mark a purchase as a gift to avoid duty?+

No. Customs declarations must be accurate. We can compare lawful shipping options but will not misdescribe the goods or their value.
