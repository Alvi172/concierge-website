Choose a destination around your taste and budget. Retailer examples are independent suggestions, not affiliations or guaranteed stock.

01 / EVERYDAY

The high street

Boots, Superdrug, Marks & Spencer, Next, John Lewis, TK Maxx and Primark: beauty, family essentials, clothing and homeware. Start with a fixed product brief to keep fees proportionate.

02 / DESIGNER OUTLET

Bicester Village

Designer outlet shopping by appointment. Several boutiques offer their own virtual shopping; our added value is a cross-store brief, approved comparisons and onward coordination.

03 / DEPARTMENT STORES

Selfridges & Harrods

Selfridges has London, Birmingham and Manchester stores. Harrods’ landmark department store is in Knightsbridge; H beauty operates elsewhere. Plan around the specific department and store.

04 / BRITISH HERITAGE

Jermyn Street & beyond

Shirts, tailoring and shoes around Jermyn Street; bespoke tailoring appointments on Savile Row; luxury boutiques around Bond Street. Measurements and fittings need careful planning.

05 / BEAUTY & OCCASIONS

A thoughtful edit

Build a shade wishlist, gift hamper or wedding shortlist before a store session. Perfumes, aerosols and certain cosmetics can have shipping restrictions; check the route first.

06 / TECHNOLOGY

A smarter total cost

Compare the exact iPhone or laptop model, unlocked status, warranty, sales tax, customs, insurance and compatibility. A lower shelf price in the USA may not mean a lower delivered price in Pakistan.

About iPhones, eSIMs and Pakistan

An eSIM changes how a plan is installed; it is not a blanket exemption from device registration or import taxes. Temporary visitor registration is conditional and time-limited. We will not advertise permanent “PTA-free” handset deals. Foreign roaming arrangements also need provider-specific checks.
