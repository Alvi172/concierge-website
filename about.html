EXPERIENCE THAT UNDERSTANDS YOU

Different journeys.
A shared purpose.

Concierge.pk brings together the combined experience of professionals whose lives and work in the UK span the past 30 years, alongside a team of professionals from around the world. Our story is shaped by the connections between where we come from, where we build our lives and the people we care about along the way.

That experience belongs to the people behind our service. It gives us a personal understanding of the questions, practical challenges and emotional moments that come with living across borders.

We know that a request is rarely just a task. An airport pickup can be a parent’s peace of mind. A birthday delivery can say “I wish I could be there”. A supplier visit can be the first step towards a business someone has spent years imagining.

WHY WE CARE

Because being far away
should not mean feeling helpless.

The idea behind Concierge.pk begins with a familiar feeling: “If only I had someone there who understood.”

Perhaps your child is arriving in the UK for the first time. Perhaps you live overseas and want to make Eid special for family in Pakistan. Perhaps you have found a promising product but need someone to visit the supplier, ask the right questions and help you understand your next step.

We created Concierge.pk to bring thoughtful human support to those moments. We care about the small details because they often carry the greatest meaning: understanding a family’s preferences, checking an arrival time, explaining a quotation clearly or keeping you informed when plans change.

Our passion is simple: helping people stay connected to their families, their ambitions and their roots, wherever life takes them.

PAKISTAN AT HEART. THE WORLD WITHIN REACH.

A connection
that works both ways.

For people in Pakistan, we help make the UK feel more approachable: settling in, thoughtful shopping, business representation and practical coordination. For people overseas, our Pakistan desk helps bring home a little closer through gifts, celebrations, family requests and sourcing support.

From exploring manufacturing options in Sialkot, Faisalabad, Lahore and Karachi to planning a meaningful celebration, each request starts with listening. We clarify what you need, where support is available and which people or specialist providers should be involved.

Our international team brings different perspectives to a shared purpose. Assignments beyond the UK and Pakistan are arranged individually, with location, availability, responsibilities and costs confirmed before you commit.

OUR WAY OF WORKING

Personal attention.
Clear expectations.

01

We listen before we plan

Tell us the story behind your request, your timing and your budget. We help turn that into a practical brief, with room for the details that make it yours.

02

You stay in control

We agree the scope, service fees and external costs before work begins. Purchases and changes need your approval, with updates and receipts where relevant.

03

The right help for the task

You have an agreed point of contact. Where specialist advice or licensed services are required, we clarify the provider’s role and any separate charges.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

A thoughtful service.
A relationship worth building.

We want you to feel comfortable asking questions, confident about what has been agreed and informed throughout the process. We value honest conversations, cultural understanding, discretion and realistic promises.

Some requests are joyful. Others are practical, urgent or deeply personal. We approach each with care, and we will tell you clearly when something needs a different specialist or falls outside what we can arrange.

Whether you need help once or a familiar point of contact for the years ahead, we would love to understand what would make your life easier.
