THE IDEA

Your person
on the other side.

Founded by A. Alvi, a Pakistani resident of the UK for more than sixteen years, Concierge.pk starts with a simple understanding: crossing borders is easier when someone can help with the practical details.

The aim is to connect Pakistani families, students, shoppers, creators and businesses with thoughtful, clearly scoped support in the UK, and selected international assignments when availability allows.

This is a founder-led launch. We do not claim a nationwide team, permanent overseas offices or partnerships that have not yet been agreed.
