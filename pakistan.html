Before we start.

Can I order from the UK, Europe, USA, Canada or the Gulf?

Yes, you can enquire from overseas. Tell us the destination city in Pakistan and your preferred contact time. We confirm the service, payment arrangements and delivery coverage before accepting.

Can you keep a gift or party a surprise?

We agree a safe contact and delivery plan with you. Some deliveries need a recipient telephone number or building-access contact. We never guarantee secrecy where a supplier must contact the recipient.

What if the exact gift is unavailable?

We send alternatives with prices. No substitution is made without approval. Tell us whether timing, brand, colour or budget matters most.

Will I receive photos?

Where agreed and permitted, we provide item or delivery evidence. Recipient images and social-media posts require consent. Hall of Fame features are optional.

How do I pay and who buys the items?

Your quote explains the service fee, purchasing budget, supplier payments and cancellation terms. We may ask you to pay a supplier directly or approve a purchase budget before work begins.

Can you handle emergencies or medical decisions?

No. Our family support is planned, non-emergency assistance. Contact local emergency services or the relevant qualified professional for urgent or clinical matters.
