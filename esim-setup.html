Before you install

Confirm your exact model and country variant supports eSIM and is carrier-unlocked. Use reliable Wi-Fi. Keep your QR code private and accessible on another screen. Ask when this specific package starts: installation and first-network-connection activation rules vary. Do not assume every package starts on arrival.

iPhone

Open Settings → Mobile Data / Cellular → Add eSIM → Use QR Code. Scan the supplied code and follow the prompts. Label the line “Concierge Travel”. If scanning is impractical, use Enter Details Manually with the activation details supplied for your order. Do not guess activation codes.

Samsung Galaxy

Open Settings → Connections → SIM manager → Add eSIM. Choose the QR-code option and scan your supplied code. Follow the prompts. Menu labels vary by software version; use manual activation details only when supplied for your plan.

Other Android phones

Look in Settings → Network & Internet → SIMs for Add SIM or Set up an eSIM. The exact route differs by manufacturer. If the option is absent, check your model documentation and carrier lock status before purchasing.

When you arrive

Enable the travel eSIM and select it for mobile data. Turn data roaming on for the travel line if your plan instructions require it. Turn home-line data roaming and automatic mobile-data switching off to reduce accidental charges. Keep your home line for calls only if you accept its roaming charges. Follow the supplied APN instructions; leave settings unchanged unless instructed.

After your trip

Switch mobile data back to your home line. Disable the travel line when finished. Do not delete a profile you may still need: reuse, reinstallation and transfer vary by package. Ask before deleting or resetting the phone.
