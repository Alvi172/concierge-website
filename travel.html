In simple words

We prepare a trip plan based on your budget, dates and needs. You receive a practical route, accommodation options and activity suggestions. At launch you book directly with suppliers or an appropriate travel provider; our planning fee is not the holiday price.

What you receive

A personalised day-by-day itinerary, accommodation options, transport guidance and booking links or a specialist handoff. Availability and prices must be rechecked when booking.

Which package should I choose?

  1. Choose City Edit for one city and a trip of up to three days, with one revision.
  2. Choose Family Journey for up to six travellers, one country and up to seven days. It includes two revisions and one pre-departure call.
  3. Choose Signature Multi-city for up to three cities and fourteen days, with two calls and two revisions. A complex itinerary can cost more after scope is agreed.

IN PRACTICE

A brief, made real.

A seven-day family itinerary costs £299 for planning. If the family later chooses £1,800 of flights and accommodation, that supplier cost is separate. No flight or hotel is reserved by paying only the planning fee.

Before we begin

  • Planning fees buy research and itinerary work. Flights, hotels, insurance, visas and activity costs are separate.
  • At launch, customers book directly or with an appropriately protected travel provider. Do not assume separate invoices remove package-travel obligations.
  • Concierge.pk is not represented as ATOL-protected or a visa adviser. Entry permissions and supplier cancellation terms remain the traveller’s responsibility.

READY TO ASK?

Tell us these details.

Traveller numbers/ages; destinations and dates; approximate trip budget; pace; room needs; interests; and dietary or access preferences. Share sensitive details only through an appropriate private channel if necessary.

Suggested enquiry timing: 2–4 weeks before travel, and 4–8 weeks for complicated or peak-season trips. No visa outcome or appointment is promised.

How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Are flights, hotels or visas included?+

No. The package buys itinerary research and the stated revisions. Flights, hotels, travel insurance, attraction tickets, visas and meals are extra.

Can you book everything for me?+

Booking assistance depends on the accepted scope and appropriate travel-provider arrangement. At launch the default is direct customer booking or a suitable specialist. We do not present Concierge.pk as an ATOL-protected holiday organiser.

Can you plan for halal food, children or sensory preferences?+

Yes, describe the practical preferences so they inform the plan. Supplier suitability must be confirmed. We do not provide clinical advice or guarantee that a location will meet every access need.

What counts as a revision?+

A revision is one consolidated set of changes within the original destination, duration and traveller scope. Changing countries, doubling the trip or starting again with new dates can require a new quote.
