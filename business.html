You give us a written brief and we attend as your external representative. We can ask your questions, present material you approve, observe what is openly available and send useful notes. You make the commercial decisions and sign your own contracts.

What you receive

A factual written summary, answers obtained to your agreed questions, permitted photos where useful and clear next actions. We separate observations from anything we could not verify.

Which package should I choose?

  1. Choose Meet & Report for one straightforward meeting of up to 60 minutes, with preparation and notes within two total work hours.
  2. Choose Field Desk for a visit or research task needing up to four hours including preparation and reporting. Travel is separately quoted.
  3. Choose UK Business Desk when you have regular follow-ups. The monthly fee reserves ten hours across up to three active briefs, not unlimited meetings.

How your time allowance works

Preparation, calls, messages, coordination and reporting all use the stated work allowance. Calendar duration is different: “six hours over seven days” means six work hours in total spread across that week.

If a task needs more time, we explain the extra fee and ask before continuing. A third-party approval or delay does not mean we can guarantee the outcome.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Can you negotiate or sign a contract on my behalf?+

We can communicate your approved position and report the response. We do not sign or commit your business unless a separate lawful authority and scope have been reviewed and accepted. Ordinary packages do not include this authority.

Does a supplier visit prove the supplier is genuine?+

No single visit proves that. We can document what was observed and what public records show. Financial, legal, technical and fraud due diligence may need qualified specialists.

Can you interview a job applicant for me?+

We can ask a pre-agreed, appropriate question set and provide factual notes with the candidate’s knowledge. You make hiring decisions. Background investigations, discriminatory screening or unapproved recording are not included.

What legal or civil work is available?+

Document organisation and appointment coordination can be scoped. Solicitors, notaries and other appropriate professionals handle advice, court work and certification. We do not act as your lawyer or immigration adviser.
