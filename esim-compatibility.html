Check the device you actually own

A model family alone is not proof of compatibility. Country variants, operator locks and firmware can change support. Look for an EID in your device information and an Add eSIM option in mobile settings. These are useful checks, not a guarantee that a particular travel plan will activate.

iPhone

Many iPhone XS, XR and later models support eSIM, but regional exceptions apply. Check Settings → General → About for model details and Carrier Lock; “No SIM restrictions” indicates an unlocked iPhone. Confirm your exact variant using Apple’s official guidance.

Samsung Galaxy

Selected Galaxy S, Z Fold, Z Flip, Note and A models support eSIM. Do not assume every phone in a series does. Check Settings → Connections → SIM manager for Add eSIM, then confirm the precise model and region against Samsung’s list.

Pixel, Motorola, Xiaomi, Honor, Sony and other devices

Support varies across models and markets. Send the exact model number, country of purchase and the name of your original carrier. Check the manufacturer’s product specifications; we will confirm the proposed plan before payment. Wi-Fi-only tablets do not gain cellular capability by installing an eSIM.

A quick compatibility enquiry

Send your model and country variant, destination, travel dates, and whether the phone is carrier-unlocked. Do not send full IMEI/EID numbers, passwords or QR codes in your initial enquiry. A screenshot can be useful after you hide personal identifiers.
