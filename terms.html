CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

A well-defined brief.
A better experience.

Draft service principles for the launch preview. Final booking terms will identify the contracting business and be supplied before payment.

1. Enquiry and acceptance

A website enquiry is not a booking. Availability, lawful scope, fees and the task owner are confirmed in an accepted written quote. We may decline work outside our competence or capacity.

2. Prices and spending

GBP rates are proposed launch fees for the stated scope. The final quote must show the consumer’s tax-inclusive total and separate goods, expenses and supplier charges. No extra work or substitutions without approval. Any shopping advance is limited to the approved task, reconciled with receipts and unused funds returned through the agreed lawful route.

3. Third parties

Each quote identifies whether you contract directly with a supplier and whether Concierge.pk is acting as your agent or as a seller. Delivery, transport, travel booking, professional advice and warehouse arrangements have their own stated terms. We do not imply protection or approvals that have not been obtained.

4. Cancellation and refunds

Applicable statutory cancellation rights remain intact. Where work is requested during a statutory cooling-off period, the required express request and disclosures must be obtained before starting. A blanket “non-refundable” label does not override your rights. Authorised work, bespoke goods and external bookings require their applicable specific terms.

5. Service limits

No guarantee of bank, marketplace, immigration or government approval; stock availability; NHS dental appointments; savings; business results; or customs clearance. Public-service application support is optional. No fake documents, borrowed accounts, payment routing for unrelated transactions, surveillance or undeclared goods.

6. Timing and permissions

You supply accurate details, authority and access permissions. We agree reporting milestones and time limits. International assignments require country-specific entry/work checks; arrivals and transport use appropriately licensed providers.

7. Concerns and disputes

Raise an issue promptly through the verified contact supplied with your quote. The final terms will identify the legal business, geographical address, complaint route, governing law and applicable consumer remedies. Liability limits, if any, require proper review and cannot exclude non-excludable rights.

8. Enquiry-only status

The live Google form accepts enquiries only. No payment, service contract or paid membership is accepted through it. Business identity, contact details, fulfilment capacity and final terms must be provided and agreed before any paid assignment.
